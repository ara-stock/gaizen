## Welcome

GAIZEN FINANCE is an investment blog focused on **high-probability, long-term investing**.

The name GAIZEN comes from the Japanese word **蓋然 (gaizen)**, meaning probability or likelihood. Rather than predicting markets, the goal is to make consistently sound choices and let compounding do its work.

## What This Blog Covers

- **Index investing**: S&P500, All-Country (オルカン), and why long-term accumulation wins
- **Japanese stocks**: Trading companies, financials, and high-dividend growth stocks
- **FIRE planning**: How to calculate your number and build toward financial independence
- **Dollar-cost averaging**: Daily vs. monthly vs. lump-sum — what the data says
